# CHAPTER 16 — SCENARIO & FORESIGHT STRUCTURE

## Chapter Introduction

With Layer 2 — Execution System (Performance) complete, we now enter Layer 3 — Adaptive System (Longevity). This layer addresses how the institution survives shocks and adapts to change. The first and most foundational chapter of this layer is Scenario & Foresight Structure.

Scenario and foresight structure is the design of structured anticipation of uncertainty to protect long-term viability and strategic positioning. It answers the fundamental questions:

- What plausible futures could materially affect the institution?
- What risks are low-probability but high-impact?
- What structural assumptions may fail?
- What macro shifts could redefine value?
- What technological discontinuities threaten relevance?
- What policy shifts could reshape the landscape?
- What optional moves are pre-designed?

Execution optimizes the present. Foresight protects the future.

Without foresight, institutions react. With foresight, they pre-position. The institution that anticipates change can adapt proactively. The institution that is surprised by change must react reactively—and often too late.

This chapter provides the architecture for designing scenario and foresight structure that is systematic, integrated, and actionable. It addresses the full spectrum of foresight considerations—from scenario planning and macro risk scanning to early warning indicators, strategic pivot playbooks, and black swan stress mapping.

### Why Scenario & Foresight Structure Matters

Scenario and foresight structure is the future intelligence architecture of the institution. It determines:

- **What plausible futures could materially affect the institution:** The range of possible futures.
- **What risks are low-probability but high-impact:** The black swan risks.

- **What technological discontinuities threaten relevance:** The technologies that could disrupt.
- **What policy shifts could reshape the landscape:** The policy changes that could reshape.
- **What optional moves are pre-designed:** The moves that are pre-planned.

## 5. Design Principles

### Principle 1: Multiple Plausible Futures Must Be Considered

**What does this principle mean?**

The institution must consider multiple plausible futures—not just one forecast.

**Why is this a principle?**

Single-forecast thinking creates fragility. Multiple futures enable preparation.

**How is it implemented?**

| Implementation Method | How It Works |
|---|---|
| **Scenario planning** | Develop multiple scenarios. |
| **Alternative futures** | Consider alternative futures. |
| **Regular review** | Review scenarios regularly. |
| **Board engagement** | Engage the board in scenario planning. |

**What are the trade-offs?**

| Trade-off | Consideration |
|---|---|
| **Completeness vs. efficiency** | Multiple scenarios require resources. |
| **Preparation vs. cost** | Scenario planning has a cost. |

**What happens if it is violated?**

The institution is surprised by change.

## 14. MICOS Scorecard

### What is the purpose of this scorecard?

The MICOS Scenario & Foresight Structure Scorecard is a self-assessment tool for evaluating the maturity and durability of an institution's scenario and foresight structure.

### How is it used?

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| **1** | Complete the scorecard for each dimension. |
| **2** | Identify areas where the institution scores "No" or "Partial." |
| **3** | Prioritize remediation based on risk and impact. |
| **4** | Track progress over time. |
| **5** | Use the scorecard for due diligence and investor communication. |

### A. Structural Awareness

| Criterion | Question | Significance | "Yes" Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Assumptions documented** | Are assumptions documented? | Documentation enables testing. | Core assumptions are documented. |
| **Multiple scenarios modeled** | Are multiple scenarios modeled? | Multiple scenarios provide coverage. | Multiple scenarios are modeled. |
| **Downside stress included** | Is downside stress included? | Downside testing reveals vulnerabilities. | Downside scenarios are included. |
| **Tail risks examined** | Are tail risks examined? | Tail risk examination prevents surprises. | Tail risks are examined. |

### B. Integration

| Criterion | Question | Significance | "Yes" Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Linked to capital planning** | Is foresight linked to capital planning? | Integration ensures action. | Foresight is linked to capital planning. |
| **Linked to governance review** | Is foresight linked to governance review? | Governance ensures oversight. | Foresight is linked to governance. |
| **Linked to incentive calibration** | Is foresight linked to incentive calibration? | Integration ensures alignment. | Foresight is linked to incentives. |
| **Linked to exit strategy** | Is foresight linked to exit strategy? | Integration ensures exit readiness. | Foresight is linked to exit. |

### C. Monitoring

| Criterion | Question | Significance | "Yes" Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Early warning indicators defined** | Are early warning indicators defined? | Definition enables monitoring. | Early warning indicators are defined. |
| **Ownership assigned** | Is monitoring ownership assigned? | Ownership ensures accountability. | Monitoring ownership is assigned. |
| **Signals regularly reviewed** | Are signals regularly reviewed? | Review enables action. | Signals are reviewed regularly. |
| **Response triggers documented** | Are response triggers documented? | Documentation enables rapid response. | Response triggers are documented. |

### D. Institutional Readiness

| Criterion | Question | Significance | "Yes" Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Board participation active** | Is board participation active? | Active participation ensures governance. | The board participates in foresight. |
| **War-gaming conducted** | Has war-gaming been conducted? | War-gaming reveals vulnerabilities. | War-gaming has been conducted. |
| **Strategic pivots drafted** | Are strategic pivots drafted? | Drafting enables rapid response. | Strategic pivots are drafted. |
| **Annual refresh institutionalized** | Is annual refresh institutionalized? | Institutionalization ensures continuity. | Annual refresh is institutionalized. |

### Maturity Rating

| Level | Description | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — No formal foresight** | There is no formal foresight. | No scenario planning. No anticipation. |
| **1 — Basic forecasting** | Basic forecasting exists. | Some forecasting but no scenario planning. Limited anticipation. |
